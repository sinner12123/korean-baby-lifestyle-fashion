# Fixed visual system

Use this reference for every generation or edit performed by the skill.

## Creative direction

Warm Korean baby-and-family lifestyle editorial with the product clarity of ecommerce photography. The child feels observed during real play rather than directed on a fashion set. The result is clean, tender, airy, modern, and practical—not saccharine, luxury-glossy, theatrical, or vintage-heavy.

## Subject and casting

- Fictional East Asian baby or toddler; choose one explicit age band per campaign: 6–12 months, 12–24 months, or 2–4 years.
- Natural round child proportions, healthy skin texture, soft dark hair, bright but unforced expression.
- Candid micro-expressions: quiet curiosity, shy smile, delighted laugh, focused play, mild pout.
- Styling remains age-appropriate. No makeup, adult posing, adult accessories, or sexualized framing.

## Palette

- Base: warm white, oatmeal, cream, pale beige, light natural wood.
- Accents: butter yellow, dusty peach, blush pink, sage, mint, baby blue, lavender, muted navy.
- Patterns: restrained gingham, tiny flowers, dots, berries, soft stripes, or small geometric motifs.
- Maintain low-to-medium saturation, soft highlight roll-off, neutral-to-warm white balance, and clean skin tones. Avoid muddy beige casts and candy-neon color.

## Light and texture

- Large-window daylight or open shade; soft directional light from 30–60 degrees to camera.
- Bright exposure without clipped white garments; gentle shadows retain volume.
- Natural cotton, modal, rib, gauze, mesh, terry, and knit texture must remain legible.
- Very light fine grain is optional. Avoid heavy CCD artifacts, aggressive bloom, orange filters, crushed blacks, hard flash, and plastic skin.

## Environment

- Indoors: white linen bed, cream rug, pale plaster wall, light wood furniture, sparse nursery objects, woven basket, soft quilt or simple toy.
- Outdoors: quiet garden path, pale wall, trimmed grass, airy terrace, or calm neighborhood in open shade.
- Backgrounds feel lived-in but edited. Keep visual noise and saturated props away from the garment.
- One or two small props maximum; prefer wood, fabric, paper, or muted-color toys.

## Camera language

- Camera near the child's eye/chest height, usually 1.5–4 m away.
- Natural 50–85 mm full-frame equivalent for undistorted proportions; 35–50 mm only for wider environmental moments.
- Full body, three-quarter, seated medium, and detail crops. Leave breathing room and avoid cutting through hands, feet, necklines, or garment features.
- Moderate depth of field: subject and key garment details sharp, background softly separated but still readable.
- Slight motion in hair or limbs is welcome; face and garment stay clear.

## Composition ratio

For a default 8-image campaign:

1. clean full-body hero;
2. seated or kneeling play;
3. walking, crawling, or turning motion;
4. smiling medium portrait showing neckline;
5. quiet candid side view;
6. fabric/closure detail;
7. wider environmental story frame;
8. optional caregiver interaction with the adult secondary and minimally visible.

## Non-negotiable garment fidelity

Preserve garment category, silhouette, hem length, neckline, sleeve shape and length, closure count and placement, waistband height, pocket location, seams, trim, print scale and placement, fabric texture, and color family. Do not add bows, collars, ruffles, pockets, logos, or decorative stitching absent from the reference.

## Controlled variables

The garment, season, activity, setting, props, crop, aspect ratio, and expression may vary. Identity, age band, photographic treatment, palette logic, camera behavior, naturalism, and garment-fidelity rules stay fixed within a campaign.
